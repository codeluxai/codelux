# Security

Codelux keeps your code index, notes and session history on your machine. Team
data is end-to-end encrypted: the server stores ciphertext only.

If you believe you have found a security issue, please **do not open a public
issue**. Write to **hello@codelux.ai** with:

- what you found and how to reproduce it,
- the Codelux version (Account tab in the app) and your OS,
- whether the issue affects the app, the website or the team service.

You will get an acknowledgement within 3 business days and a fix or a
mitigation plan within 30 days for confirmed issues. We credit reporters who
want to be credited.
